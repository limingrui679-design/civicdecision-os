# Tier-D scenario-template catalog

These are twelve non-duplicative designs. Each is bound once to each of eight cities; the resulting 96 executions are not counted as 96 unique methods.

| # | Template | Suite | Completion strategy | Intended claim |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `deep.public-service.total-demand.v1` | public-service-operations | total-demand | A reproducible planning comparison conditioned on observed aggregate request counts and explicit hypothetical action parameters. |
| 2 | `deep.public-service.seasonal-staffing.v1` | public-service-operations | total-demand | A seasonal workload planning exercise, not a forecast of staffing productivity or a personnel prescription. |
| 3 | `deep.public-service.sanitation-workload.v1` | public-service-operations | category-demand | A taxonomy-explicit workload subset and simulated planning comparison; category matching does not verify sanitation conditions. |
| 4 | `deep.climate.heat-service-surge.v1` | climate-disaster-resilience | total-demand | A compound planning stress test that juxtaposes, but does not causally link, request workload and gridded heat. |
| 5 | `deep.climate.rainfall-continuity.v1` | climate-disaster-resilience | total-demand | A rainfall-context planning exercise without claiming that precipitation caused the observed requests. |
| 6 | `deep.housing.request-triage.v1` | housing-land-use-regeneration | category-demand | A request-taxonomy planning subset, not a measure of housing quality, violations, tenure outcomes, or regeneration impact. |
| 7 | `deep.health.environmental-request-screen.v1` | population-health-environmental-exposure | category-demand | An environmental request-label screen with population and climate context, not an exposure, diagnosis, health-risk, or causal-effect estimate. |
| 8 | `deep.infrastructure.maintenance-portfolio.v1` | infrastructure-finance-asset-risk | category-demand | A request-based maintenance planning proxy, not an asset-condition survey, capital budget, engineering design, or financial return. |
| 9 | `deep.equity.area-balance.v1` | behavioral-policy-equity | total-demand | A distributional planning diagnostic over operational area labels, not a demographic equity finding or individual behavioral inference. |
| 10 | `deep.mobility.accessibility-request.v1` | mobility-accessibility-operations | category-demand | A request-label workload plan only; it does not measure route accessibility, travel time, disability experience, or compliance. |
| 11 | `deep.equity.causal-service-effectiveness.v1` | behavioral-policy-equity | required-causal-design | An explicit insufficient-evidence release unless a dated intervention, comparison group, outcome panel, and identification diagnostics exist. |
| 12 | `deep.mobility.real-time-reroute.v1` | mobility-accessibility-operations | required-network | An explicit insufficient-evidence release until versioned routable networks, service calendars, disruptions, and impedance validation are bound. |

## Universal boundary

- A service request is not a verified incident, need, exposure, outcome, or person.
- Proposed coefficients are not observed or causal effects.
- Optimized portfolios are mathematical planning outputs, not city recommendations.
- Negative packs are required outputs when causal or network evidence is absent.
