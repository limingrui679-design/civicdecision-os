# Community code of conduct

CivicDecision OS welcomes technically rigorous disagreement about data semantics, analytical
methods, claim boundaries, urban context, and software design. Participation requires respect for
the people doing that work and for communities represented in public data.

## Expected conduct

- Critique evidence, code, methods, and claims rather than a person's identity or competence.
- State uncertainty and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Protect personal, confidential, restricted, and security-sensitive information.
- Do not infer individual behavior, need, health, risk, or intent from aggregate public records.
- Give contributors and upstream data providers accurate credit.
- Accept correction when a claim exceeds its evidence.
- Keep review comments specific enough to reproduce or act on.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained personal attacks,
deliberate misrepresentation, and disclosure of exploitable vulnerabilities in public threads are
not permitted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report security vulnerabilities through GitHub private vulnerability reporting. For conduct
concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the contact method on the owner's GitHub
profile. Do not include unnecessary personal data.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when needed to
protect contributors and reviewers. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately when appropriate, and should not be represented as legal or institutional findings.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review sessions, and other
project spaces controlled by the maintainer.
