05 / Interpretation contract

What this system proves—
and what it does not.

Scale is reported as artifact counts, city assignments, source units, scenario executions, and reproducible runs. Those numbers are meaningful only alongside their scope and limitations.

Supported

Claims the repository can substantiate

  • Versioned public artifacts can be hash-verified.
  • Typed models reject inconsistent catalog states.
  • Declared analytical runs can be reproduced.
Not claimed

Claims deliberately withheld

  • No assertion of production deployment or institutional adoption.
  • No assertion of external peer review, certified governance, or real users.
  • No causal effect claim from descriptive public-data cases.
  • No conversion of national or grid-point context into local truth.
